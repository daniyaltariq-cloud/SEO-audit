Canonical Tags Pointing to Non-200 URLs
Canonicalization · Duplicate Content · Index Signals
20pages affected
▾

Consequences

A canonical pointing to a 404 or 301 sends Google a broken instruction. It either ignores the tag entirely — causing duplicate content issues — or follows the chain and lands on an unexpected destination. Either way, link equity isn't consolidating as intended and Google may choose the wrong version to rank. 7 pages point to 404 dead ends, actively destroying their own SEO signals.

How to Fix

  • For canonicals → 404: restore the target URL, or update the canonical to a live 200 URL
  • For canonicals → 301: skip the redirect chain — point canonical directly to the final destination URL
  • For www vs non-www discrepancies (www.codup.co vs codup.co) — ensure consistent canonical domain
  • Use Yoast/RankMath canonical field per post; verify with Screaming Frog after updating

Expected Results

Correct canonicals consolidate link equity to the right URL, improving rankings for those specific pages within 2–4 weeks. Duplicate content ambiguity is resolved. Pages that were competing against their own variants will now have a single clear winner that Google can rank confidently.
